Privacy Policy

Last updated 20 November 2026. Operated by Dab Media LLC (“dabberz”, “we”).

This describes what the platform actually does with data, rather than what is conventional to say. If something here is unclear, ask — the answer should be checkable against the source.

Two different kinds of data

Account data
What we hold about you as a customer: your email address, your sign-in identity, which apps you have installed, billing records, and a log of actions taken on your account.
Your application data
What lives inside the apps you install — your form submissions, your contacts, your documents. We store and back this up so your apps run. We do not mine it, sell it, or use it to train anything.

What we collect

  • Identity. Your email address and authentication details, handled by our identity provider. We do not store your password.
  • Installation records. Which apps you installed, when, their addresses, and the history of changes made to them.
  • An audit trail. Actions taken on your account — installs, changes, promotions, rollbacks, sign-ins — with who performed them. Secret values are never written to it; only their names.
  • Operational logs. Requests to the platform, errors, and resource use, kept to run and debug the service.
  • Billing information. Processed by our payment processor. We do not store full card numbers.

Who else processes it

Running this requires other companies. These are the ones that can see data, and what each one sees:

  • OVH — the physical servers in the United States where your apps and databases run.
  • Cloudflare — DNS and the network edge. Sees traffic to your apps in transit.
  • Clerk — identity and sign-in. Holds your email and authentication records.
  • Anthropic — when you ask an agent to change an app. See below, because this one works differently.
  • An object storage provider and an email provider — for file storage and for mail your apps send.

Agents and your AI subscription

When you ask an agent to change one of your apps, that request and the relevant parts of your app’s code are sent to Anthropic to be processed.

This happens under your Claude subscription, not ours. We do not resell AI capacity, and your prompts are governed by your own agreement with Anthropic rather than by us. We pass the request through; we do not retain the conversation beyond what is needed to show you the history of your own workspace.

An agent works against a copy of your application data, so what it can reach is a fork rather than your live records.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your data, to anyone, in any form.
  • We do not use your application data to train models.
  • We do not run third-party advertising or analytics trackers on this site or in the control panel.

Getting your data out

Every app on the platform can export everything it can import, in a documented format, at any time, without asking us. This is a product commitment rather than a courtesy: a platform meant to free people from locked-in software should not be locked-in software.

You can also request a copy of your account data, or its deletion, by emailing us. We will respond within 30 days.

How long we keep it

Application data is kept while your installation exists, plus up to 30 days in backups after you delete it. Account and billing records are kept for as long as required for tax and accounting purposes. Operational logs are kept for up to 90 days.

Security, stated honestly

Data is encrypted in transit. Secrets are encrypted at rest and are never written to logs or the audit trail. Databases are provisioned per installation with their own credentials, so one installation cannot read another’s.

dabberz is early software operated by a small company. We publish the parts that are not finished rather than implying everything is. If you are evaluating it for data you cannot afford to lose, read the known gaps first and ask us anything that matters to you.

Children

This is a business product and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect their information.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, export or delete your personal data, and to object to certain processing. Email us and we will honour them; we do not require you to use a particular form to ask.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that materially affects you, we will email you before it takes effect.
