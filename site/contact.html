Asking for access

dabberz is early and onboarding is by conversation rather than a signup form. Tell us roughly what you’d want to run and we’ll tell you honestly whether it’s a fit yet. If it isn’t, we’ll say so rather than put you on a list.

Which app should exist next

Forms is the only app in the catalog today. CRM and help desk are the two we hear asked for most, and the order isn’t settled — so if there is a tool you’d genuinely move off, say which one and what you pay for it now. Email with dabberz NEXT APP in the subject and we’ll tell you when it ships, whether or not you end up a customer.

Security

If you believe you have found a security issue, email the address above with dabberz SECURITY in the subject — that mailbox serves several things, and the subject is what gets it read first. Please give us a reasonable chance to fix it before publishing. We will not pursue anyone acting in good faith, and we will credit you if you would like that.

The platform is open source, so you can also read the code and open an issue — but please use email rather than a public issue for anything exploitable.

Privacy and data requests

To request a copy of your data, a correction, or deletion, email the address above. We respond within 30 days and do not require a particular form. See the Privacy Policy.
